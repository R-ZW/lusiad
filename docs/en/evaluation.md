## Stages and participants

The evaluation had four stages: selection of the methodological approach, technical validation by the author, two pilot sessions, and fifteen main sessions. Main sessions took place from 8 to 12 November 2026. Participants were recruited by convenience, mainly at UNIPAMPA's Alegrete campus and IFFar's Uruguaiana campus. They needed to be able to read and make simple changes to Java programs and have no previous use of Lusiad. Sessions were individual and moderated.

## Protocol

Each main session began with consent and a participant profile questionnaire. Participants then performed five tasks in a prepared Java project. These involved inserting and editing model elements, creating a commit and branch, restoring a state, comparing versions, and examining individual changes. The final questionnaire contained ten System Usability Scale items, three perceived-usefulness items, and two declared-intention items. The script encouraged participants to describe difficulties and perceptions. The pilot sessions led to browser-based access, guided tutorials, and researcher assistance during tasks. Pilot data was excluded from the analysis.

The [Portuguese instruments](../../evaluation/instruments/) reproduce the applied forms. The consent form mentions three tasks although the applied task form lists five. The discrepancy is retained as a historical fact.

## Measures and limits

The study recorded ten factual answers from tasks T3–T5, SUS scores, and distributions for usefulness and intention items. Factual answers provide a partial view of interaction. Session records contextualize them. There was no time-per-task measure, unassisted-performance classification, or controlled comparison with another IDE. Comments and excerpts were interpreted descriptively. The sample and prepared project delimit generalization.
